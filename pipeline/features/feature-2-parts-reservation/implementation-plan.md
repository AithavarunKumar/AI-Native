## Notes

- **Highest-risk task: T-02.** The `synchronized` check-and-reserve is the only concurrency control in the system. A mistake here allows stock oversubscription. T-02 output must be code-reviewed before T-03 begins.
- **Business decision gate:** T-02 must not start until the partial-stock policy is answered and recorded in `pipeline/decisions/feature-2-decisions.md`.
- **Out of scope:** cancellation lifecycle integration, backorder creation, stock consumption at job completion — these remain open questions per `requirements.md`.
